Planet Cracker Pro – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

Planet Cracker Pro ("the game") is published by Puffy Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what data the game and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: benjamingranville@gmail.com.

Summary

The game has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads, may show interstitial ads, offers optional in-app purchases, and measures how the game is played so we can improve it.

Third parties

  • AppLovin (MAX) and the ad networks it mediates, which may include Google AdMob and Unity Ads – AppLovin privacy policy, Google privacy policy, Unity privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics – Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing – Google privacy policy.

These partners act under their own privacy policies. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal data for money; sharing identifiers with advertising partners for personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some laws, and you can opt out as described below.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before using your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's shop.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads. On iOS you can refuse tracking when asked, or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. To ask about data held by our partners, or to make an access or deletion request, email us at the address above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help have it removed.

Retention

Analytics data is kept by Google for the retention period set in Firebase (up to 14 months). Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies. Purchase records are kept by Google Play.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
