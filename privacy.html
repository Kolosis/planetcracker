Planet Cracker – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

Planet Cracker ("the game") is published by Puffy Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what data the game and its partners collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: benjamingranville@gmail.com.

Summary

The game has no accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads, may show interstitial ads, offers optional in-app purchases, and uses advertising and purchasing services as described below.

Third parties

  • Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads when advertising is enabled: LevelPlay mediates ads supplied by Unity Ads, ironSource and Google AdMob. They process device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and honor privacy choices – Unity privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): AdMob supplies ads through LevelPlay and processes device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP collects and stores advertising consent choices where required. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Unity In-App Purchasing – Unity privacy policy. This provides the game’s connection to app-store purchases.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – Google privacy policy, Firebase privacy information. It receives the game’s usage events to help us understand and improve the game. In the current mobile versions, gameplay analytics remain enabled while Firebase advertising features are disabled.
  • Apple App Store (purchases on iPhone) – Apple privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing (purchases on Android) – Google privacy policy.

These partners act under their own privacy policies. Data is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal data for money; sharing identifiers with advertising partners for personalised ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some laws, and you can opt out as described below.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the game asks for your consent before using your data for personalised ads. Google UMP presents the applicable consent form. Revisit it through PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's shop, then Manage consent choices when available.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: PRIVACY CHOICES is available in every region. Choose Do not sell or share my data to send the restriction to LevelPlay and enable Google restricted data processing. Existing opt-outs are preserved. This does not remove ads or all necessary ad processing. Removing this extra limit does not override a separate consent refusal or your phone’s tracking settings.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads. On iOS you can refuse tracking when asked, or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Deleting data: uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device. To ask about data held by our partners, or to make an access or deletion request, email us at the address above.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help have it removed.

Retention

Game progress and purchase entitlements are kept on your device until removed. Support emails are retained for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. Advertising and purchasing services retain data under their own policies; store transaction records may remain available after you uninstall the game.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
